# Planora Business Requirements

## Objective

Planora reduces the time and control risk between financial source data, management understanding, decision, action and measured outcome while preserving Excel interoperability.

## Stakeholders

CFOs need trusted outlook and decisions; FP&A leaders need governed planning and variance workflows; analysts need traceable models and efficient Excel ingestion; business leaders need accountable actions; data stewards need definitions, quality and certification; auditors need immutable evidence.

## Business outcomes

- Shorter budget, forecast, variance investigation and reporting cycles.
- Higher reconciliation, data quality, metric certification and forecast accuracy.
- Faster scenario-to-decision cycle with recorded authority.
- Higher action completion and expected-to-realized financial impact.
- Lower manual adjustment and remapping effort without forcing Excel replacement.

## Mandatory controls

Tenant isolation, permission-based RBAC, immutable audit, exact financial arithmetic, source lineage, version immutability, human approval of material financial state and decisions, safe spreadsheet handling, and governed AI recommendations are release requirements rather than optional features.
